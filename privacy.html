This Privacy Policy applies to the app Terraforming Personal Board (the "App").

Data Collection

The App does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All information you enter into the App is stored locally on your device and never leaves it.[reference:1]

Permissions

The App does not request any sensitive permissions. It does not access your camera, contacts, location, files, or any other personal data.

Internet Connection

The App works entirely offline. It does not connect to any server or third-party service. No analytics, no tracking, no advertising.

Third-Party Services

The App does not use any third-party services that could collect information about you. It does not use any tracking technologies (such as cookies, fingerprinting, or web beacons).[reference:2]

Children's Privacy

The App does not collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.
